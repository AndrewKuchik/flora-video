Idea and script

How deeply I get involved in the idea is up to you. Three formats:

No script0 €

You already have a plan: what we shoot, where and how. I come, set the light and shoot strictly to your plan.

Involvement100 €

You lead the idea, I help turn it into a shooting plan and suggest solutions for the shots. On set I handle the filming and the light.

Full service300 €

I take the idea on. We discuss the business goal, within a week or two I send a concept and options, and we refine it to the final. Locations and approvals are on me too.

Terms

  • Prices are final, PVN is not added.
  • Payment. Script 100 % upfront. Filming: 20 % deposit, 80 % on the shoot day. Editing: 50 % at the end of the shoot day, 50 % on delivery.
  • Timing. The finished video usually within 2 weeks of the shoot. If you need it faster, we agree separately.
  • Travel. In Riga 20 €. Up to 20 km from Riga 50 €, up to 50 km 100 €, further by arrangement.

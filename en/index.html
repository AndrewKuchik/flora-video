3

Script and pre-production

How deeply I'm involved in the idea is up to you. Three formats:

No script0 €

The client handles all the prep: references, script, shot plan. On set I direct: I follow the ready plan, set the light and run the process. We shoot strictly to your script.

Involvement100 €

You handle the organisation, locations and script and gather references. I give notes and suggest what works better. On set I cover the same: I run the filming to the plan and set the light.

Full service300 €

I take on everything. First we discuss your wishes and ideas, then within a week or two I send references and several concept options. We refine over a few rounds to the final. Scouting locations and all approvals are on me too.

7

Terms

  • Prices are final, PVN is not added.
  • Payment. Script 100 % upfront. Filming: 20 % deposit, 80 % on the shoot day. Editing: 50 % at the end of the shoot day, 50 % on delivery.
  • Timeline. Script about a week, two at most. The finished clip usually within 2 weeks after filming. If you need a rush, we arrange it separately.
  • Shoot days. A clip can be filmed in one day or a few hours, or across several days.
  • Travel. In Riga 20 €. Up to 20 km from Riga 50 €, up to 50 km 100 €, further by arrangement.
  • Revisions. 2 rounds included. If no notes arrive within a week of the sent version, it is considered accepted.
  • Offer valid for: 30 days.
